# Trademarks

Extrudo's code is free software ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE), [`NOTICE`](NOTICE)): you
may use, change and share it, and publish your own version. The **name and the
logo are not part of that license.** They tell people that a program, a site or
a download comes from this project, and that only works if nobody else uses
them for theirs.

## What this covers

- The name **Extrudo**, and names close enough to be confused with it.
- The **Extrudo logo** (the dashed sketch square with the amber solid) in any
  form, colour or size, and the app icons made from it.
- The domain **extrudo.org** and its subdomains. Only the project runs sites
  there.

The marks belong to Zoltan Fekete, the project's maintainer.

## What you may do without asking

- Say "Extrudo" to talk about the project, write about it, teach it, review it
  or link to it.
- Share unmodified copies of the official releases and of this repository.
- Say that your project is **"based on Extrudo"**, **"a fork of Extrudo"** or
  **"compatible with Extrudo files"**, as long as your own name comes first and
  nothing suggests the project made or endorses it.
- Show the logo next to a link to extrudo.org or the repository, unchanged.
- Run Extrudo for yourself, your school or your company, under the Extrudo
  name, as long as you don't change it.

## What needs a different name and logo

If you change the code and publish the result (a fork, a hosted copy, a
package, an app-store build), give it **your own name and your own icon**. Don't
call it Extrudo, don't use the logo, and don't use a name or icon that looks
like them. That way nobody blames this project for your changes, or yours for
ours. Packaging the official code for a distribution with only the changes the
distribution needs (paths, build flags) is fine under the Extrudo name.

The marks also can't be used for a product name, company name, domain or
social-media account, or in a way that suggests the project endorses you.

## Rebranding a fork

- Replace the image files listed in [`NOTICE`](NOTICE) §5 and the drawing in
  `apps/web/src/design-system/Logo.tsx`.
- Change the product name (`apps/web/index.html`,
  `apps/web/public/manifest.webmanifest`, `apps/desktop/package.json` and
  `apps/desktop/electron-builder.yml`).
- Point the addresses at your own: `SITE_URL`, `APP_URL`, `EDGE_URL` and
  `CONTACT_EMAIL` are build-time variables (`apps/site/addresses.ts`).

## Asking

For anything not covered here, write to hello@extrudo.org. The legal basis is
section 7(e) of the GPL v3, which allows a license to decline trademark rights;
this page states how the project uses that.
