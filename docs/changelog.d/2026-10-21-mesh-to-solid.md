- 2026-10-21 · **Mesh to Solid**: an imported STL, 3MF or OBJ mesh becomes a solid with real faces (Solid › Modify, or Convert to Solid… on the body), so it fillets, shells and takes sketches like any body, or tick As solid when importing (#95). Prismatic recognises planes, cylinders, cones, spheres and tori (exact where the whole mesh fits, otherwise a hybrid that keeps the unfit parts as facets; Auto tolerance); Faceted keeps every flat facet as a face (up to 5,000). While its dialog is open, the mesh shows which parts are planes (green), curved surfaces (blue) or fit nothing (red), and a Result line says what the solid will have.
