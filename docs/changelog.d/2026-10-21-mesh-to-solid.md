- 2026-10-21 · **Mesh to Solid**: an imported STL, 3MF or OBJ mesh becomes a solid with real faces (Solid › Modify, or Convert to Solid… on the body), so it fillets, shells and takes sketches like any body (#95). While its dialog is open, the mesh shows which parts are planes (green), cylinders (blue) or fit nothing (red), and a Result line says what the solid will have.
